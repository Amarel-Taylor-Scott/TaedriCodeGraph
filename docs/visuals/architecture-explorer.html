Why this is not ordinary EAV

  • Typed: every payload declares a wire family and schema.
  • Versioned: descriptor meanings cannot be reinterpreted in place.
  • Provenanced: identical content can dedupe while attempts stay distinct.
  • Scoped: validity, policy, audience, environment, and purpose remain explicit.
  • Indexable: recipes promote selected values into typed physical lanes.
  • Negative-space aware: failed, stale, unknown, withheld, and not-computed are rows—not emptiness.
